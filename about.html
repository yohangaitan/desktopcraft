About DesktopCraft

A blog built for people who care about how their screen looks.

What Is DesktopCraft?

DesktopCraft is a blog dedicated to desktop customization, wallpaper selection, and setup aesthetics. Every guide here is written to be practical — not just inspirational. The goal is to give you the specific tools, steps, and decisions you need to build a desktop that genuinely looks the way you want it to look, on any operating system.

The topics covered range from beginner-friendly introductions (how to choose a wallpaper that matches your color scheme) to more advanced setup guides (Linux desktop customization with Picom and Conky). Whether you use Windows, macOS, or Linux, there is something here for you.

Who Writes This?

DesktopCraft is written and maintained by a developer and desktop setup enthusiast who has spent years customizing Linux environments, experimenting with different aesthetic directions, and building tools for the setup community — including YohaWallpaper, a free wallpaper platform featuring a hand-curated selection of wallpapers sourced from public repositories, as well as original animated wallpapers created exclusively for the platform.

The content on this blog comes from hands-on experience — every tool mentioned has been tested, every technique described has been applied to a real setup. There are no paid placements or sponsored recommendations. If something is worth recommending, it is here because it actually works.

What You Will Find Here

  • Step-by-step desktop setup guides for Windows, macOS, and Linux
  • Wallpaper selection and color coordination tips
  • Reviews and guides for free customization tools
  • Aesthetic-specific guides (dark, cyberpunk, anime, minimal, and more)
  • Resolution and technical guides for getting the best image quality

Our Philosophy

Good desktop setups are not about having the most widgets or the most complex configuration. They are about intentionality — making deliberate choices about every visual element and having them work together cohesively. A three-element setup that is perfectly coordinated will always look better than a ten-element setup that is not.

The guides on DesktopCraft are written with this principle in mind. We favor quality over quantity, and explain the reasoning behind each recommendation so you can adapt it to your own taste rather than just copying a result.
